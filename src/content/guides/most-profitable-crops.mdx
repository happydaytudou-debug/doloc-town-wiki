## Before Comparing Crops

Naming one crop “most profitable” requires matching sale prices, growth cycles, seasons, and other conditions from the same game version. The linked community pages do not provide that complete comparison. <SourceRef id="S2" /> <SourceRef id="S4" />

<GuideWarning>This page intentionally contains no crop prices, rankings, or recommendations. Publishing them now would turn a search intent into an unsupported game fact.</GuideWarning>

## What a valid comparison needs

- A dated game version and exact crop data source.
- Purchase cost, sale value, growth duration, and repeat-harvest behavior where applicable.
- Any season, gene, processing, or progression condition that changes the result.
- A clearly stated formula so readers can reproduce the comparison.

## Profit is not the same as overall value

Crop Tier List covers broader usefulness, while this page is limited to an economic comparison. Gene System and Ranching Guide may introduce separate variables; include them only when their values are visible in the same version.

## Check in Your Version

Compare exact, dated tables or the values shown in your current game rather than relying on a community landing page. Record the version and formula whenever you share a result.
