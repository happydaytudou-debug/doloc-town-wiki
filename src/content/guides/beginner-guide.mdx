## What This Guide Covers

This page covers early goals, basic farming, gathering, building, and upgrade decisions. The Steam storefront and Community Guides index offer broad orientation, but neither provides one definitive opening route. <SourceRef id="S1" /> <SourceRef id="S4" />

<GuideWarning>Opening objectives and costs can differ by version or progress. Follow the objective text and tutorials shown in your game instead of treating any day-by-day route as universal.</GuideWarning>

## A safe way to begin

Use the game’s current objectives and tutorials as the authority while playing. Before spending a limited resource or committing to an upgrade, check the in-game description and save state available in your version. Record any step that behaves differently so it can be matched to a dated source later.

## Check in Your Version

- The order of early objectives and unlocks.
- Which tools or upgrades should be prioritized.
- Exact costs, resource quantities, and timing.
- Version-specific mistakes that can delay progression.
