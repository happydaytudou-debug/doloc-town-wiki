## What This Guide Covers

Fish records are most useful when they include location, weather or time conditions, and version context. The linked community pages are broad starting points and do not provide a complete location table. <SourceRef id="S2" /> <SourceRef id="S4" />

<GuideWarning>Use the fishing information shown in your current game. This page does not assign a fish to a location or availability condition without a specific record.</GuideWarning>

## Details to Record

A reliable record needs the exact fish name, area label, fishing spot, any visible time or weather condition, version date, and a reproducible source. Uses should be sourced separately from location data.

## Reader workflow

Use the fishing information shown by the current game and keep observations version-specific. Map offers navigation context, while Recipes should be compared separately with the ingredients displayed in game.

## About Location Tables

A location table is useful only when each row has a clear fish name, place, condition, and version. Broad community landing pages are not treated as support for individual rows.
