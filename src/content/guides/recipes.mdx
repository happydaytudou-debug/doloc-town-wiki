## Recipe coverage

The supplied matrix reserves this page for cooking and crafting recipes, ingredients, unlocks, and uses. The community hub and guide index are discovery inputs rather than a verified recipe database. <SourceRef id="S2" /> <SourceRef id="S4" />

<GuideWarning>No ingredient list, quantity, station requirement, unlock condition, or output effect is verified in the imported package.</GuideWarning>

## What a trustworthy recipe entry needs

Each future entry should identify its exact in-game name, category, ingredients and quantities, required station, unlock condition, output, version, and a source that visibly supports those fields.

## How to use this page now

Check the recipe interface in your current game version before collecting or spending materials. Item pages for Metal Frame, Old Chips, and Old Engine Cores remain separate so that a crafting relationship is not assumed without evidence.

## Verification priorities

The first evidence pass should establish terminology and unlock requirements. Only then can recipes be grouped or compared without presenting incomplete lists as exhaustive.
