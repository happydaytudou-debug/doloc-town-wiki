## Recipe coverage

This page covers cooking and crafting recipes, ingredients, unlocks, and uses. The community hub and guide index are broad references rather than a recipe-by-recipe database. <SourceRef id="S2" /> <SourceRef id="S4" />

<GuideWarning>Confirm ingredients, quantities, station requirements, unlock conditions, and output effects in the recipe interface for your version.</GuideWarning>

## Details to Check

For any recipe, check the exact in-game name, category, ingredients and quantities, required station, unlock condition, output, and version. A broad landing page is not support for those individual fields.

## Before You Craft

Check the recipe interface in your current game version before collecting or spending materials. Item pages for Metal Frame, Old Chips, and Old Engine Cores remain separate so that a crafting relationship is not assumed without evidence.

## Version Notes

Terminology and unlock requirements may change between versions. Treat any recipe list as version-specific and do not assume that an incomplete list is exhaustive.
