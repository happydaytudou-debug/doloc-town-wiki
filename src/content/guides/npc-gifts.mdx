## Standard for a future gift entry

Each claim should identify the NPC, exact item, observed reaction, game version, and whether the result came from the game itself or a player report. Numeric relationship changes require especially clear evidence.

## Practical caution

Confirm an item name and any in-game feedback before repeating a gift strategy. Community agreement can help locate evidence, but repetition does not convert a report into an official rule.
