## Before You Give a Gift

Gift advice may involve preferences, reactions, relationship changes, and common mistakes. The linked player-community pages are broad discussion indexes rather than character-by-character records. <SourceRef id="S3" /> <SourceRef id="S5" />

<GuideWarning>Confirm character preferences, reactions, relationship values, birthdays, schedules, and locations in your current version before following a gift routine.</GuideWarning>

## Details Worth Checking

Check the NPC, exact item, observed reaction, game version, and whether a result came from the game itself or a player report. Numeric relationship changes need especially clear support.

## Practical caution

Confirm an item name and any in-game feedback before repeating a gift strategy. Community agreement can help locate evidence, but repetition does not convert a report into an official rule.
