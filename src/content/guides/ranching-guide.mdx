## Ranching scope

This page covers animals, products, facilities, and version context at a general level. The storefront and community hub offer broad background rather than a detailed ranching table. <SourceRef id="S1" /> <SourceRef id="S2" />

<GuideWarning>Confirm animal availability, care schedules, facility requirements, product values, timing, and costs in your current version.</GuideWarning>

## Before You Invest

Distinguish animal acquisition, housing, daily care, products, processing, and economics. Check every cost, interval, output, and unlock condition against the current game version.

## Planning context

Beginner Guide provides broader progression context, while Most Profitable Crops offers a separate economic framework. Do not assume one activity outperforms another without comparable values from the same version.

## Reader caution

Use the current in-game facility and animal interfaces for immediate decisions, particularly where resources cannot be easily recovered.
